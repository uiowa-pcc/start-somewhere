Start Somewhere

Not sure what you want to do yet? You do not need a career goal to get started. Choose a few things that sound important, interesting, or worth trying. We’ll connect those reactions to University of Iowa career areas and example careers you can explore next.

This is a starting point, not an assessment. Your results are meant to give you a few places to begin—not tell you what you should do or what you should major in. Career examples in this tool are grounded in University of Iowa Career Community resources. Notice what makes you curious, what does not, and what you want to learn more about.
