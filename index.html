How were these suggestions generated?

The interest and activity choices are cross-checked against O*NET’s RIASEC career-interest framework and specific interest areas. The work-priority choices are based on O*NET work-value categories. Your values are saved for reflection but do not rank career areas, because work conditions can vary widely within the same field. Career directions are ranked by direct matches to the topics and activities you selected, and the examples and links are aligned with current University of Iowa career resources. This is not a formal or validated career assessment.
